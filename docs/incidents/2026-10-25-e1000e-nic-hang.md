I notice that all of my services had suddenly gone dark in the middle of the day.  I have no scheduled restarts or shutdowns for the machine or VMs in place.  The machine was still running, ethernet lights were still blinking, and the VMs were still up, but all network-related services were offline.  The pve01 logs showed a repeated "HARDWARE UNIT HANG" message every 2 seconds for the past 30 minutes from the time I noticed.  After much research online, I discovered it was likely due to an error in the e1000e NIC driver.  There were many bug reports of this being common when run with newer Linux kernels, such as the Ubuntu Server LTS 24.04 I was running, as well as Proxmox 9.2.21.

I dismissed it the first time, solving it by just unplugging and replugging in the physical ethernet cable.  However, the issue returned later that day.  This time, I opted for a more permanent solution, where I disabled TSO (TCP Segmentation Offload):

iface nic0 inet manual
    post-up /sbin/ethtool -K nic0 tso off
